# Panelumi 0.14.0

A window capture now takes what was actually on screen when the shortcut was
pressed, popups included.

## Screenshot

- A window pick lands on the menus, popovers and floating panels that are there:
  an app's open menu, a status-item panel, or a widget that floats itself above
  everything. Each is listed and captured at the press, before Panelumi
  activates and they close, and cropped to the part that is actually drawn — a
  panel that lives inside a much larger transparent window no longer brings the
  desktop around it into the shot.
- A window that closes between the press and the click is cut from the frozen
  frame instead of ending in "the selected window could not be captured".
- A window that draws nothing is dropped rather than saved as an empty image.
- The Dock is a window pick now: its window covers the whole display rather
  than the strip it draws, so the pick is the strip and its pixels come from the
  frozen frame. A hidden Dock is not offered.
- The menu bar still takes a region, as before; it was never a window pick. The Dock's window covers the
  whole display rather than the strip it draws, and the menu bar is the 33
  points the pointer rests on right after opening a panel from it; drag a region
  over either.

## Fixed

- `--window-check` no longer crashes when it runs without an application.
